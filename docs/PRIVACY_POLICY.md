## The short version

- You can use core RigReceipts **without an account**. In that mode your records
  stay on your device.
- If you **create an account**, your records back up to your private, per-user
  space so they sync across devices.
- **Receipts and documents are read on your device.** We don't upload an image
  unless you save the record.
- We collect **pseudonymous usage and crash data** to keep the app working and
  improve it. We never put your document contents, amounts, names, or addresses
  into analytics or crash reports.
- We **do not sell your data**, we **do not show third-party ads**, and we **do
  not track you across other apps or websites**.
- Community rate posts are **privacy-safe**: your name, load numbers, addresses,
  contacts, and documents are removed, and your identity is never shown.

## Who this applies to

This policy covers the RigReceipts mobile app for iOS and Android. It does not
cover the App Store, Google Play, or third-party services you reach through
links, which have their own policies.

## What we collect and why

**Account information.** If you create an account, we collect your **email
address** to sign you in and to sync your data. Account creation is optional.

**Content you create.** The records you enter or capture — receipts and rate
confirmations (including their images), expenses, loads, mileage, cost profiles,
and rate checks. When you are signed out, this stays on your device. When you are
signed in, it is stored in your private, per-user space so it can sync and
restore. We use it only to provide the app's features to you.

**Camera and photos.** When you scan a receipt or a rate confirmation, the camera
opens only for that action and the text is recognized **on your device**. A
captured image is stored (in your account space) only if you save the record. We
don't use your camera or photos for tracking or advertising.

**Community Rate Board posts (optional).** If you choose to post a rate, we
publish a **privacy-safe snapshot** — the metro-level lane, equipment, rate
status, and the pay figures you choose to include. Before anything is shared we
remove your name, load and document numbers, exact addresses and times, and all
contacts. On the public board your identity is never shown; posts carry a
rotating, per-lane alias that cannot be traced back to you or followed from lane
to lane. You can remove your posts at any time, and reports and blocks are
handled by our moderation process.

**Usage analytics.** We collect **pseudonymous** product-usage events (for
example, that a rate check was completed, or a paywall was viewed) together with
low-cardinality context such as your selected role, subscription tier, and
equipment type. Events are tied to your account identifier or, when you are
signed out, to a random device identifier. We **never** include document
contents, amounts tied to you, names, or addresses in analytics.

**Diagnostics and crash data.** We collect crash reports and basic diagnostics to
find and fix problems. These are configured to exclude personal information and
never include your document contents.

**Purchases.** If you subscribe, we and our purchases provider record your
**subscription status and plan** so we can unlock features and restore purchases.
Your payment is processed by Apple or Google — **we do not receive your card
number or bank details.**

## What we do not collect

We do **not** collect precise or coarse **location**, your **contacts**,
**browsing history**, **health** data, or **advertising identifiers**. We do not
build advertising profiles.

## How we use your information

- To provide, sync, and restore the features you use.
- To operate the Community Rate Board and its safety and moderation controls.
- To keep the app reliable and secure and to fix problems.
- To understand which features are used so we can improve the app.
- To process subscriptions and restore purchases.
- To comply with law and enforce our terms.

We do not use your information for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

## When we share information

We share information only with **service providers** who process it on our behalf
under contract, and only as needed to run the app:

| Provider       | Role                                | Where                 |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Supabase       | Account, database, and file storage | Canada (ca-central-1) |
| PostHog        | Product analytics                   | United States         |
| Sentry         | Crash reporting and diagnostics     | United States         |
| RevenueCat     | Subscription management             | United States         |
| Apple / Google | Payment processing and app delivery | Per their policies    |

We may also disclose information if required by **law**, to protect rights and
safety, or as part of a **business transfer** (with notice where required). We
**do not sell** your personal information and we **do not share** it for
cross-context behavioral advertising.

## Data retention

We keep your records for as long as your account is active. Content stored only
on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. Analytics and
crash data are retained for a limited period for security and product analysis.
When you delete your account, your associated records are deleted (some minimal
data may be retained where required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention
purposes).

## Security

Data is **encrypted in transit** (HTTPS/TLS). Your account records are protected
by row-level security so that you can only access your own data. Receipt and
document text recognition runs **on your device**. No method of storage or
transmission is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

## Your choices and rights

- **Use the app without an account** to keep records on your device only.
- **Access and export**: your records are visible in the app; contact us to
  request an export.
- **Correct**: edit your records in the app at any time.
- **Delete**: delete individual records in the app, or delete your account to
  remove your associated data. Uninstalling the app removes device-only data.
- **Community posts**: remove any rate card you posted at any time.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights (below). To exercise
any right, contact **jburwell@rigreceipts.com**.

### California (CCPA/CPRA)

In the past 12 months we have collected the categories described above
(identifiers such as email; commercial information such as your subscription
status; user content; and internet/usage activity such as analytics). We collect
it for the business purposes described above. **We do not sell or share personal
information** as those terms are defined under the CCPA/CPRA, and we do not use
sensitive personal information to infer characteristics. California residents may
request access, deletion, and correction, and will not be discriminated against
for exercising these rights.

### EU / UK (GDPR / UK GDPR)

RigReceipts is the data controller. Our legal bases are: **performance of a
contract** (providing the app and account features), **legitimate interests**
(keeping the app secure and improving it, balanced against your rights), and
**consent** where required. You have the rights of access, rectification,
erasure, restriction, portability, and objection, and you may lodge a complaint
with your local supervisory authority. Because our providers operate in Canada
and the United States, your data may be transferred there under appropriate
safeguards.

## Children

RigReceipts is a business tool for professional drivers. It is not directed to
children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children
under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction).

## International users

We and our service providers process data in **Canada** and the **United
States**. By using RigReceipts you understand your information may be processed in
these locations, which may have different data-protection laws than your own.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. We will change the "Last updated"
date above and, for material changes, provide notice in the app. For the
Community Rate Board specifically, you will be asked to review and agree again if
the community terms change.
