Know what the load really pays.

A good loaded rate can still be a bad load. RigReceipts folds in deadhead, fuel, and your real operating costs, then tells you what is actually left.

In development — coming to iOS and Android

What it does

Rate Check

Enter the offer and the miles. See your all-mile RPM against your break-even and your target before you accept.

Receipts

Scan fuel, tolls, repairs, and lumper receipts. Text is read on your device, and you confirm every field before it saves.

Mileage

Loaded, deadhead, and business miles tracked separately — because every empty mile comes out of the same pocket.

Loads

Keep the rate confirmation, BOL, POD, and receipts together in one packet, from booked through paid.

Money owed

Detention, lumper, and reimbursements tracked until they actually land.

Road Grade

An honest read on rate, fuel, deadhead, paperwork, and money owed. Missing data is never counted as a failure.

What it is not

RigReceipts is not a load board, broker, or dispatch service, and nothing in it can be booked or bid on. It does not give financial, accounting, tax, or legal advice — every figure is an estimate based on the numbers you enter.
