What to include

The more of this you send, the faster we can fix it:

  • Your phone and OS version (for example, iPhone 14 / iOS 18).
  • What you were doing when it happened, step by step.
  • What you expected versus what actually happened.
  • A screenshot, if the problem is something you can see.

Please do not email rate confirmations, bills of lading, or other documents unless we ask — we do not need your paperwork to fix a bug.

Common questions

Billing, refunds, and cancelling

Subscriptions are processed by Apple and Google, not by us. Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple account settings or Google Play account settings. Refund requests are handled by Apple or Google under their policies. If a purchase did not unlock, email us and we will help sort it out.

Deleting your account or data

You can delete your account from inside the app under Settings. See delete your account for exactly what gets removed and how to request it by email instead.

Your numbers look wrong

Rate checks, break-even figures, and RPM are estimates based on the costs and miles you enter. If a figure looks off, check your cost profile in the RPM Coach first — an empty or stale profile is the usual cause. If it still looks wrong, email us the numbers you entered and what you expected.
