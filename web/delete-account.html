Delete your account

Remove your RigReceipts account and all data synced to it. This is permanent.

Fastest: delete in the app

If you have RigReceipts installed and can sign in, you can delete your account yourself:

  1. Open RigReceipts and go to the Reports tab.
  2. Tap Account & data.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

Request deletion without the app

If you can't access the app, send us a deletion request. We verify that the request comes from the account owner before deleting.

This opens your email app with a pre-filled message to privacy@rigreceipts.app. Prefer to write us directly? Email that address with the subject "Account deletion request."

What gets deleted

Whether you delete in the app or by request, we remove:

  • your account and sign-in;
  • your loads, receipts, expenses, mileage, rate checks, and cost profile;
  • any rate cards you posted to the Community Rate Board;
  • the receipt and document images stored in your account.

Deleting your account removes all of the above together. Community rate ranges are built from anonymized, aggregated data with no link to you, so there is nothing personal to remove there. We may retain minimal records where the law requires it (for example, tax or fraud-prevention obligations), as described in our Privacy Policy.

How long it takes

In-app deletion is immediate. Emailed requests are completed within 30 days of verifying ownership, and usually much sooner.
